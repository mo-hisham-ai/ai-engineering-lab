Nile Tech Employee Handbook

Working hours: The office is open from 9 AM to 5 PM, Sunday to Thursday. Employees may choose flexible start times between 8 AM and 10 AM, as long as they complete 8 hours of work each day.

Remote work: Employees can work from home up to two days per week. Remote days must be agreed with the team lead at least one day in advance. During remote days, employees must be available on the company chat between 10 AM and 4 PM.

Annual leave: Every full-time employee receives 21 days of paid annual leave per year. Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks before the planned dates. Unused leave days can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days.

Sick leave: Employees get 10 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is required if the sick leave lasts more than two consecutive days. Please notify your manager before 9 AM on the first day of absence.

Equipment: Each new employee receives a laptop, a monitor, and a headset. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported to the IT department within 24 hours. Personal devices may not be connected to the internal network.

Learning budget: Every employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 dollars for courses, books, and conferences. Purchases must be approved by the manager and receipts submitted within 30 days.
